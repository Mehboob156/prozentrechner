# Prozentrechner

A practical German percentage calculator with formulas, examples, and step-by-step explanations.

## What Can You Calculate?

### Prozentwert

Calculate how much X percent of a given value is.

**Formula:**

`W = G × p ÷ 100`

Example:

25% of 1,000:

`1,000 × 25 ÷ 100 = 250`

### Prozentsatz

Find what percentage one value represents of another value.

**Formula:**

`p = W ÷ G × 100`

Example:

50 is what percentage of 200?

`50 ÷ 200 × 100 = 25%`

### Grundwert

Calculate the full value when you know a percentage and its corresponding value.

**Formula:**

`G = W × 100 ÷ p`

Example:

50 is 25% of what value?

`50 × 100 ÷ 25 = 200`

### Prozentuale Veränderung

Calculate the percentage change between an initial value and a final value.

**Formula:**

`(Endwert − Anfangswert) ÷ Anfangswert × 100`

Example:

A value increases from 80 to 100:

`(100 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = 25%`
